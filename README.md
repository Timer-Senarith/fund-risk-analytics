## 🚀 Featured project

### [📊 Fund Performance & Risk Analytics](https://github.com/Timer-Senarith/fund-risk-analytics)
*Do actively managed US mutual funds earn their fees?* Tests 12 active funds against passive benchmarks from 2015 to 2026.
- ETL from Yahoo Finance, the Kenneth French Data Library and FRED, with **17 automated data-quality checks**, loaded into a **PostgreSQL star schema** (Neon) and mirrored to **Snowflake**
- Fama-French 5-factor + momentum regressions with Newey-West errors, bootstrap CIs and **Benjamini-Hochberg** correction for testing 12 funds at once
- **Finding:** 9 of 12 funds had negative net alpha. The best fund's p = 0.027 rose to 0.33 after correction, so there is no reliable evidence of skill.
- `Python` `pandas` `statsmodels` `PostgreSQL` `Snowflake` `SQLAlchemy` `Tableau`
